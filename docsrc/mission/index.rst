``geometor.poincare`` unites a verbatim, philologically verified bilingual **Book Codex** of **Henri Poincaré's** *La Science et l'Hypothèse* (*Science and Hypothesis*, 1902 / 1905) with **100% exact symbolic constructions** of the Poincaré Conformal Hyperbolic Disk inside ``geometor.model``.

Core Pillars
------------

1. **Verbatim Bilingual Primary Sources**: Pair Ernest Flammarion's 1902 Paris French text (including Poincaré's critical section *Les Géométries de Hilbert*, omitted from the 1905 London translation) with W. J. Greenstreet's 1905 London translation and G. B. Halsted's 1905 New York translation across a synchronized 4-track reader (*English · French (1902) · Summary · Notes*).
2. **Poincaré's Translation Dictionary in Exact Algebra**: Realize Poincaré's profound insight—that every theorem of Bolyai–Lobachevsky hyperbolic geometry translates into an exact theorem of Euclidean circle inversion and orthogonal circles inside ``geometor.model``—using exact SymPy rationals and quadratic radicals with zero floating-point approximation.
3. **Noetic & Axiomatic Dialogue Across GEOMETOR**: Situate Poincaré's geometric **conventionalism** (*les axiomes ne sont que des définitions déguisées*) directly alongside Euclid (*Elements* Postulate V), Hilbert (*Grundlagen der Geometrie*), Bertrand Russell (*An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry* & the 1898–1900 *Revue de Métaphysique et de Morale* debate), Peter Hoenen S.J. (*De Noetica Geometriae*, 1954), Felix Klein (*Erlanger Programm*), Pappus (cross-ratio invariance), John Casey (orthogonal circles), Émile Lemoine (*Géométrographie*), and Norman Wildberger (*Universal Hyperbolic Geometry*).
