Unit II: Non-Euclidean Geometries — Summary
===========================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Digest of Part II, Chapter III (*Les géométries non euclidiennes*)

.. contents:: In This Summary

   :depth: 2

1. The Collapse of the Parallel Postulate Chimera
-------------------------------------------------

Distinguishing analytical *a priori* common notions ($A = B \wedge B = C \Rightarrow A = C$) from genuinely geometric postulates, :person:`Poincaré <poincare>` recounts how :person:`Lobachevsky <lobachevsky>` (1829–1840) and :person:`János Bolyai <bolyai>` (1832) ended twenty-one centuries of vain attempts to prove :term:`Euclid's Fifth Postulate <postulatum-euclide>` by assuming multiple parallels through an exterior point and developing a contradiction-free hyperbolic geometry in which:

* The angle sum of every triangle is strictly less than $\pi$, with :term:`angular defect <defaut-angulaire>` $\Delta = \pi - (\alpha + \beta + \gamma)$ proportional to area;
* Non-congruent similar figures cannot exist (absolute length scale);
* :person:`Riemann <riemann>` (1854/1867) subsequently constructed spherical/elliptic geometry by abandoning both Postulate V and uniqueness of lines through antipodal points, yielding unbounded yet finite space with zero parallels and triangle angle sums exceeding $\pi$.

2. Beltrami's Pseudosphere & Poincaré's Translation Dictionary
--------------------------------------------------------------

How do we *prove* that infinite deduction in Lobachevsky's system can never hit a hidden contradiction? :person:`Beltrami <beltrami>` (1868) embedded two-dimensional constant-negative-curvature regions on pseudospherical surfaces, and Poincaré provides an elementary three-dimensional (and planar disk/half-plane) **model-theoretic proof of relative consistency** via his two-column :term:`Translation Dictionary <dictionnaire-poincare>`:

* Hyperbolic **space / plane / straight line** $\longleftrightarrow$ interior above/within a :term:`fundamental horizon <plan-fondamental>` $\Omega$, spheres/circles cutting $\Omega$ **orthogonally**;
* Hyperbolic **angle** $\longleftrightarrow$ ordinary Euclidean angle (conformal invariance);
* Hyperbolic **distance** $\longleftrightarrow$ natural logarithm of the projective :term:`cross-ratio <rapport-anharmonique>` $\chi(P, Q; U, V)$ with the ideal intersections $U, V \in \partial\Omega$.

Any contradiction derived from Lobachevsky's axioms would translate word-for-word into a contradiction among theorems of ordinary Euclidean circle geometry!

3. Implicit Axioms, the Fourth Geometry, Lie Groups & Hilbert's Continuum
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Critique of Mill & Superposition**: Answering :person:`John Stuart Mill <mill>`, Poincaré exposes the circularity of defining geometric equality via rigid superposition and isolates the implicit axiom of rigid-body mobility (defining the straight line as the fixed axis of an invariable rotation).
* **The Fourth (Indefinite / Lorentzian) Geometry**: Allowing local rotation while denying that a ray can rotate continuously into its opposite ray yields a coherent :term:`Fourth Geometry <quatrieme-geometrie>` in which **a real straight line can be perpendicular to itself** (isotropic null line).
* **Sophus Lie & David Hilbert**: By :person:`Sophus Lie <lie>`'s group theorem, requiring free mobility of rigid figures restricts constant-curvature metric geometries to a finite family (excluding generic variable-curvature Riemannian metrics), while :person:`Veronese <veronese>` and :person:`Hilbert <hilbert>`'s *Grundlagen* (1899) generate non-Archimedean geometries over third-order continua.

4. The Conventionalist Verdict: *"Definitions in Disguise"*
-----------------------------------------------------------

Geometric axioms cannot be Kantian synthetic *a priori* judgments (for we *can* conceive and build coherent non-Euclidean geometries, whereas we *cannot* build a coherent non-inductive arithmetic), nor can they be empirical laws (for ideal lines do not exist in matter, and exact postulates would be falsified daily by imperfect solids). Therefore, **geometric axioms are conventions (*définitions déguisées*)**: guided by experience with natural solids, free subject to consistency, and judged not by truth or falsity—a meaningless question like asking whether Cartesian coordinates are "truer" than polar coordinates—but by **simplicity and convenience** (*commodité*).
